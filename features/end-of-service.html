How it works

No surprise bill when someone leaves

1

Set the terms once

Days per year for the first 5 years and after, basic or full salary, and the months of service before any is due — per employee.

2

Booked as it is earned

Each month’s gratuity is booked as an expense, so your profit is real all year, not hit at once on someone’s last day.

3

Settle in one step

On the last working day the gratuity, and any advance the employee still owes, come together in one settlement.

4

Always visible

An end-of-service sheet shows what each employee has earned so far, and it sits in their own ledger.
