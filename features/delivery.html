How it works

How to chase your daily orders and deliveries

1

Assign the route each morning

Group today's deliveries by driver and area, so every driver leaves with a clear list of stops.

2

Chase it live, not by calling around

The delivery board shows every stop's status as it happens — late ones surface on their own, flagged against the SLA.

3

Raise the invoice on the spot

The driver marks it delivered, collects cash or logs the balance, and the invoice posts automatically — no separate paperwork.

4

Review the day

SLA highlighting flags anything that ran late, so you know exactly which driver or customer to follow up with tomorrow.

Why it's better
