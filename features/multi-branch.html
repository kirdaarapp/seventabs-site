How it works

Branches that stay in sync, not in silos

1

Set your base currency once

Every branch reports back to one base currency, converted at the rate on the day of each transaction.

2

Add branches as you grow

Stock, sales and vouchers are scoped per branch from the start — nothing needs restructuring later.

3

Transfer with approval

Branch-to-branch stock transfers go through an approval step, so nothing moves untracked.

4

Revalue at period-end

Exchange rate history and period-end revaluation keep multi-currency books accurate over time.
