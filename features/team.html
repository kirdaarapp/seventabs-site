How it works

Everyone signs in as themselves

1

Invite by email

Add an Accountant, Salesman or Driver from Settings → Team — no shared logins, no shared password.

2

Each role sees its own screen

Drivers get a simplified POS and delivery view; Accountants get the full books; Owners see everything.

3

Sign in on their own device

Each team member authenticates with device-trust OTP on their own phone or laptop.

4

Everything stays in sync

Every change syncs to the cloud in real time, so the whole team is working off the same numbers.

Why it's better

No more one shared login for everyone
