How it works

From the cheque book to the bank, in four steps

1

Record the cheque

Received from a customer or given to a supplier — number, bank, amount and due date.

2

Say what it pays

Pick the invoices it pays, let it pay the oldest first, or put it against the opening balance or as an advance.

3

Clear or bounce on the day

Clearing moves the money to your bank account. A bounce puts the amount back on the invoices it paid.

4

See it on every statement

The cheque number shows on the customer or supplier statement — open it from there in one tap.
