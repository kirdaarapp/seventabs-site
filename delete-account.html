Delete Your Account

Last updated September 19, 2026

How to request deletion

SevenTabs doesn't yet have a self-service "delete my account" button in the app — to request deletion, email us directly:

  1. Send an email to support@7tabs.org from the same email address your SevenTabs account uses.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account", and tell us whether you want:
    • Your own access removed (if you're a team member of a company's SevenTabs account — an Owner can also do this faster from Settings → Team inside the app), or
    • The entire company account and all its data deleted (only the account Owner can request this).
  3. We'll confirm your identity against the account on file and reply to confirm once deletion is complete.

How long it takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days of confirming your identity.

What gets deleted

For a full company account deletion: the company profile, every team member's access to it, and the business records tied to it — customers and suppliers, invoices and purchases, inventory and stock movements, payroll and employee records, ledger entries and financial reports, and any photo attachments or notes.

For an individual team member's access removal: that person's login and their ability to access the company's data — the company's own records remain intact for the account Owner and any remaining team members, exactly the same as when an Owner removes someone from Settings → Team.

What we retain, and why

Some information isn't deleted immediately, or at all, for legitimate reasons:

  • Legal, tax, and accounting records — where law requires us or you to retain certain financial records for a minimum period, we keep what's necessary for that period even after an account is otherwise deleted.
  • Anonymized or aggregated data — information that no longer identifies you or your business isn't personal data, so it isn't subject to deletion.
  • Records needed to resolve an active dispute or enforce our Terms of Service — kept only as long as genuinely necessary for that purpose.

Questions

See our full Privacy Policy for how we handle data more broadly, or reach us at support@7tabs.org with any questions about this process.
